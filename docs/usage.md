# Usage

All actions are in the ACE interaction menu: hold the interact key (default ++left-windows++) and look at the object. Objects that have several actions have one sub menu, for example **Drawers** or **Controls**.

## Where the action is

The action is where you expect it, depending on the size of the object:

- **Small objects** (up to 1 m): in the centre of the object
- **Medium objects** (up to 3.5 m wide): centred on the object at chest height
- **Big objects** (tents, the terminal hub): near where the change happens, for a tent door that is the side with the door. If the model does not tell where, it is the side closest to you

## Antennas

Look at a satellite dish or an omni-directional antenna and choose **Connect Radio**, then the radio. **Disconnect Radio** removes the link. The link drops when the radio is more than 10 m from the antenna. Rugged communications terminals have a **Terminal** sub menu, they have to be activated with **Activate Terminal** first.

See [Antennas](objects/antennas.md).

## Weather

Look at a portable weather station and choose **Read Weather Data**, or at the windsock and choose **Read Wind**. See [Weather](objects/weather.md).

## Tents

1. Carry a packed tent item.
2. Open the ACE self-interaction menu, **Equipment** → **Set Up Tent** and pick the tent.
3. A preview follows your view. The mouse wheel rotates it, left click confirms, right click cancels.
4. Stay until the progress bar is done.

Look at a placed tent and choose **Pack Up Tent** to get the item back, you need room for it. See [Tents](objects/tents.md).

## Animations

Look at the object and use the action, for example **Open drawer 1**, **Close door** or **Extend server rack**. See [Animations](objects/animations.md) for what every object can do.

## Settings

Each addon has an **Enable ...** checkbox under its own category (**Advanced Vanilla Objects - Addon**) in the CBA settings menu, so a function you don't want can be turned off. The settings are server/mission-wide.

| Category | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Antennas | Enable antenna connections |
| Weather | Enable weather station readout |
| Tents | Enable tents, Build time |
| Animations | Enable animation actions |
| Animations (Solar) | Enable solar panel controls |
